# Deepzora brand policy

[中文](TRADEMARK_POLICY.zh-CN.md) · [Licensing](LICENSING.md)

Effective date: 2026-10-14.

## Scope and software rights

This policy describes use of the Deepzora name, logos, application icons,
wordmarks and official release identity as brands. It does not claim that any
mark is registered. It applies only to brand rights held by the Deepzora project;
Reasonix, DeepSeek, provider logos and other third-party marks belong to their
respective owners and are not licensed by this policy.

The software licenses grant rights to use, modify and redistribute the software,
including commercially. They do not grant permission to imply official status,
sponsorship or endorsement. This policy is separate from the AGPL and does not
add restrictions on copying, modifying or distributing code or assets under
their applicable copyright licenses. It does not limit fair use, truthful
descriptive references or other rights available under applicable law.

## Permitted references and redistribution

You may use the name to truthfully describe, review, discuss or document Deepzora,
link to the project, explain compatibility, and preserve required attribution.
For example, “based on Deepzora” and “compatible with Deepzora” are acceptable
when accurate and presented without suggesting endorsement.

You may redistribute an unmodified official release using its existing name
and branding, including for a fee, provided that your download or sales listing
clearly identifies you as the distributor and does not imply that your channel,
support or service is operated or approved by the Deepzora project. Follow the
software license's source and notice requirements.

## Modified versions and independent services

Use a distinct product name and your own primary logo and application icon for
modified products or independent hosted services, unless you obtain written
permission to use Deepzora branding as their product or service identity.
You may identify their origin with a secondary, factual statement such as:

> ExampleAgent is an independently maintained fork based on Deepzora. It is not
> an official Deepzora release and is not endorsed by the Deepzora project.

Keep required copyright and license notices. Renaming a fork does not remove
its AGPL or upstream MIT obligations. Names needed for technical compatibility,
such as existing command names, API identifiers or configuration paths, may
remain; retaining them does not establish official status.

## Uses requiring permission

Written permission is required to use the project's marks as the primary
identity of a modified product, independent service or business, or to claim
project sponsorship or endorsement. Do not present an independent release,
installer, update channel, domain or account as the official project, and do
not use marks in a way likely to confuse users about who produced or supports it.

Permission requests may be directed to the maintainer through
[the project repository](https://github.com/SivanCola/deepzora). Permission is
specific to the use approved; it does not grant exclusive rights or rights to
third-party brands.
